US federal tax system in 2026 — quick reference

The US tax year is the calendar year (January 1 – December 31, 2026). Most employees have taxes withheld from each paycheck through the FICA and federal/state withholding system. Self-employed workers pay estimated taxes quarterly.

2026 federal income tax brackets (single filer):

  • 10% on taxable income up to ~$11,925
  • 12% on ~$11,926–$48,475
  • 22% on ~$48,476–$103,350
  • 24% on ~$103,351–$197,300
  • 32% on ~$197,301–$250,525
  • 35% on ~$250,526–$626,350
  • 37% on over ~$626,350

FICA taxes 2026: Social Security 6.2% (up to $176,100 wage base) + Medicare 1.4%. Self-employed pay both sides (15.3% combined, with a deduction for half).

In addition to federal tax, most states levy their own income tax. Use the US Paycheck Calculator to see your combined federal + state take-home pay for any state.
