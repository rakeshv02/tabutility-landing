Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a C$105,000 salary in Ontario?

On a C$105,000 salary, an Ontario resident takes home about C$77,508 per year, or roughly C$6,459 per month, after federal and provincial income tax, CPP and EI in 2026.

How much federal tax do I pay on C$105,000 in Canada?

Federal income tax on C$105,000 (Ontario resident, 2026) is about C$14,400 after the basic personal amount, Canada employment amount, and CPP/EI credits.

Does Ontario take-home pay differ from other provinces?

Yes — every province sets its own tax rates. At the same salary, Alberta residents generally keep the most (low provincial rates, no surtax), while Quebec residents pay the most (higher rates offset by the federal abatement and social benefits).
