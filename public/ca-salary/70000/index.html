Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a C$70,000 salary in Ontario?

On a C$70,000 salary, an Ontario resident takes home about C$53,402 per year, or roughly C$4,450 per month, after federal and provincial income tax, CPP and EI in 2025.

How much federal tax do I pay on C$70,000 in Canada?

Federal income tax on C$70,000 (Ontario resident, 2025) is about C$7,626 after the basic personal amount, Canada employment amount, and CPP/EI credits.

Does Ontario take-home pay differ from other provinces?

Yes — every province sets its own tax rates. At the same salary, Alberta residents generally keep the most (low provincial rates, no surtax), while Quebec residents pay the most (higher rates offset by the federal abatement and social benefits). About this calculation

These figures use 2025 CRA federal brackets (blended 14.5% first-bracket rate after the mid-year cut), Ontario 2025 provincial rates, CPP contributions (first and second tier at 2025 maximums) and EI premiums. The Ontario health premium and surtax are included. Results are estimates and are not personal tax advice.
