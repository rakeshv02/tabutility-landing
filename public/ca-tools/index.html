Income tax by province, RRSP savings, land transfer tax, Express Entry CRS scores, GST — plus 41 take-home salary pages for Ontario and every province. No sign-up required.

Canadian taxes are unusually complicated: federal brackets, a separate layer of provincial tax for every province, and unique payroll deductions (CPP, EI) that differ by region. These free calculators cut through it — all running instantly in your browser, no account needed.

  • Land Transfer Tax — calculate LTT in Ontario, BC, Quebec and other provinces
  • Express Entry CRS — check your Comprehensive Ranking System score for permanent residency
  • GST Calculator — add or remove Canada's 5% Goods and Services Tax from any amount
  • Canada Salary Pages — Ontario baseline take-home for every C$5,000 salary bracket from C$30,000 to C$230,000; compare provinces using the calculator
