UK tax system in 2025/26 — quick reference

The UK tax year runs from 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026. Most workers pay income tax under PAYE (Pay As You Earn), where tax is deducted from salary before you receive it. The self-employed file a Self Assessment return each January.

Income tax bands 2025/26:

  • Personal allowance: £12,570 (0%)
  • Basic rate: £12,571–£50,270 (20%)
  • Higher rate: £50,271–£125,140 (40%)
  • Additional rate: over £125,140 (45%)

National Insurance (employee, Class 1) 2025/26:

  • 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270
  • 2% on earnings above £50,270

The £12,570 personal allowance tapers away for incomes above £100,000 — losing £1 of allowance for every £2 earned over £100,000, creating a 60% effective marginal rate between £100,000 and £125,140.
