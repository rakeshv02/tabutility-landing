About BC take-home pay

BC's provincial income tax starts at 5.06% on the first C$50,265 and rises through 7 brackets to 20.5% above C$265,026. The BC basic personal amount (BPA) of C$13,191 shelters that much income from provincial tax. Crucially, BC has no provincial surtax and no health premium — the Medical Services Plan (MSP) premium was eliminated on January 1, 2020.

For mid-range salaries (C$50,000–C$150,000), BC take-home pay is broadly similar to Ontario's but slightly higher because Ontario adds a surtax above certain thresholds and charges a health premium of up to C$900/year. At higher incomes (C$150,000+), BC's top rate of 20.5% is notably higher than Ontario's equivalent, so the gap narrows or reverses at the top end.
