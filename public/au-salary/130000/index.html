Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a A$130,000 salary in Australia?

On a A$130,000 salary, an Australian resident takes home about A$97,612 per year, or roughly A$8,134 per month, after income tax and the 2% Medicare levy for the 2025–26 tax year.

How much income tax do I pay on A$130,000 in Australia?

Income tax on A$130,000 (resident, 2025–26 tax year) is about A$29,788 after the Low Income Tax Offset, plus a Medicare levy of A$2,600.

Is superannuation included in this figure?

No. Employer superannuation is paid on top of your salary, not deducted from it, so it does not reduce the take-home pay shown here.

About this calculation

This calculation uses the 2025–26 tax year resident tax scale: tax-free up to A$18,200, then 16% to A$45,000, 30% to A$135,000, 37% to A$190,000 and 45% above. It applies the Low Income Tax Offset (LITO) where eligible and the 2% Medicare levy (with the low-income shade-in). Employer superannuation is paid on top of your salary and is not deducted from take-home pay. No salary sacrifice, HECS/HELP debt or private health rebate adjustments are included.
