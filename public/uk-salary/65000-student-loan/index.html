How does a £65,000 salary compare?

A £65,000 salary is well above the UK median full-time wage of roughly £37,000 (ONS, 2025). At this level you pay higher-rate tax on part of your income, so salary sacrifice into a pension is one of the most effective ways to keep more of each pay rise.

Remember that workplace pension contributions (typically 5% employee under auto-enrolment) come out before tax, reducing the income-tax bill shown here — and for student loan holders, contributions also reduce loan repayments. Benefits in kind such as a company car or private medical insurance increase your taxable income. If your tax code is not the standard 1257L, your personal allowance — and therefore your take-home — will differ from these figures.

Repayments are collected via PAYE on income above the threshold. Estimates only — not financial advice.
