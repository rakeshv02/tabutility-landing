About this calculation

Uses 2025/26 HMRC rates (personal allowance £12,570, basic 20%, higher 40%, additional 45%; NI 8%/2%) plus 2025/26 student loan thresholds: Plan 1 £26,065, Plan 2 £28,470, Plan 4 £32,745, Plan 5 £25,000 (9%), Postgraduate £21,000 (6%). Headline figures assume Plan 2 and England/Wales tax bands, no pension contributions. The Plan 4 row uses Scottish 2025/26 income tax bands, since Plan 4 borrowers studied in Scotland. Repayments are collected via PAYE on income above the threshold. Estimates only — not financial advice.
