How UK income tax works in 2025/26

Personal allowance: The first £12,570 of your salary is tax-free. This tapers to zero for incomes over £125,140.

Basic rate (20%): On income from £12,571 to £50,270.

Higher rate (40%): On income from £50,271 to £125,140.

Additional rate (45%): On income over £125,140.

National Insurance: Employees pay 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270, and 2% on earnings above £50,270.
