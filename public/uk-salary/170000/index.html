About this calculation

This calculation uses 2025/26 HMRC rates: personal allowance £12,570, basic rate 20% (up to £50,270), higher rate 40% (up to £125,140), additional rate 45%. National Insurance: 8% on earnings £12,570–£50,270, 2% above. Personal allowance tapers to zero for incomes above £125,140. The figures shown are for a standard employee with no pension contributions, student loan, or other deductions.
