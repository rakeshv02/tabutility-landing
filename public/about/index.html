🎯 The Mission

Most online tools are outdated, bloated, or designed to extract money from you. Tabutility is different. Every tool here runs entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device, there are no accounts to create, and nothing is locked behind a paywall.

The goal is simple: give you the answer and get out of the way.

  • Free forever — no hidden tiers, no trial periods
  • Private by default — all processing happens in your browser
  • No tracking — no cross-site analytics, no ad profiling
  • Actually useful — every tool solves a real problem

📖 The Story

Tabutility started on a frustrating Tuesday afternoon. I needed to check keyword density, meta description length, and readability before publishing a blog post. Semrush wanted $130/month. Every "free" alternative had a sign-up wall, a daily limit, or served me an ad before answering.

I'm Rakesh, a developer. These are arithmetic problems. So I opened a code editor and built a keyword density checker in 45 minutes. That was tool number one.

One tool became seven SEO tools. Seven became 40 finance calculators. Forty became 153 tools across salary, tax, mortgage, developer utilities, health, and more.
