Frequently asked questions

What is the take-home pay on a $95,000 salary in the US?

On a $95,000 salary, a single filer in Texas (no state income tax) takes home about $75,663 per year, or roughly $6,305 per month, after federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare for the 2026 tax year.

How much federal tax do I pay on $95,000?

Federal income tax on a $95,000 salary (single filer, 2026 tax year) is about $12,070 after the $16,100 standard deduction. Social Security adds $5,890 and Medicare $1,378.

Does this include state income tax?

No. This calculation assumes Texas, which has no state income tax, so only federal taxes and FICA apply. In a state with income tax, such as California or New York, your take-home pay would be lower.

About this calculation

This calculation uses the 2026 tax year federal tax brackets (IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, "One Big Beautiful Bill"), with the $16,100 standard deduction for a single filer. It applies Social Security at 6.2% (up to the $184,500 wage base) and Medicare at 1.45% (plus 0.9% Additional Medicare above $200,000). The state is assumed to be Texas, which has no state income tax, so no state tax is deducted — figures would be lower in a state that taxes income. No 401(k), pre-tax benefits or other deductions are included. These figures are estimates for the 2026 tax year and are not personal tax advice.
