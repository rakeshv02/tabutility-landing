Why Alberta has the highest take-home pay

Alberta's provincial income tax starts at 8% on the first C$61,200 and tops out at 15% above C$370,220. More importantly, Alberta has no surtax (unlike Ontario, which adds 20% or 36% on top of provincial tax above threshold amounts), no health premium (Ontario charges up to C$900/year) and no provincial sales tax. The provincial basic personal amount of C$21,872 also shelters more income from provincial tax than most other provinces.

The result: on a C$100,000 salary, an Albertan keeps roughly C$2,000–C$3,000 more per year than an Ontarian before factoring in the PST/HST advantage on everyday spending.
