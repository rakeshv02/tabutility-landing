## Description

*(Paste into the "Description" field — plain text, ~300 words)*

---

Tabutility is a collection of 151 free tools for anyone who wants a quick, honest answer without signing up, paying, or wading through a wall of ads.

It started because I got annoyed. Semrush wanted $130/month to do things I could compute myself. A mortgage calculator that worked without begging for my email didn't seem to exist. So I built the tools I needed — and then kept building.

**What's here:**

- **Salary & tax calculators** for UK, US, Australia, Canada, and Japan — take-home pay, income tax, national insurance, all in one slider
- **Mortgage tools** — repayment calculator, affordability checker, stamp duty / LMI estimates
- **7 free SEO tools** — keyword density, readability scorer, meta tag length checker, heading audit, word count, Flesch-Kincaid, duplicate word finder — everything Semrush charges for, free
- **Developer utilities** — Base64 encoder, URL encoder, JSON formatter, hash generator, colour converter
- **Everyday calculators** — BMI, percentage, age, discount, tip, unit converters, timezone lookup

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. No account required, no sign-up wall, no upsell. The answer appears, you close the tab.

The DIY SEO guide at tabutility.com/diy-seo/ is the other thing I'm proud of — 9 steps, 7 tools, $0, ~4 hours. It documents exactly what I do to rank pages without paying an agency.

If you find a tool wrong, slow, or missing — that's what the comments are for.
